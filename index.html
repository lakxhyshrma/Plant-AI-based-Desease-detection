💊 Plant Treatment Guide

Learn about common plant diseases and general prevention practices.

🍅

Tomato Early Blight

A fungal disease that can cause dark spots on older leaves.

General Care

  • Water near the soil.
  • Avoid prolonged leaf wetness.
  • Remove affected plant material safely.
🍅

Tomato Late Blight

A serious plant disease that can spread rapidly in suitable conditions.

General Care

  • Inspect plants regularly.
  • Avoid wet foliage where practical.
  • Seek local agricultural advice promptly.
🥔

Potato Early Blight

May cause brown spots on potato leaves.

General Care

  • Monitor older leaves.
  • Maintain appropriate plant nutrition.
  • Rotate crops where practical.
🍎

Apple Scab

A fungal disease that may cause dark, scabby lesions on leaves and fruit.

General Care

  • Clear fallen infected leaves.
  • Improve airflow around trees.
  • Consult local fruit-growing guidance.
These are general educational suggestions, not a diagnosis. Confirm the disease before choosing a treatment or pesticide.

🌱 Plant Care Tips

Simple habits for maintaining healthier plants.

💧

Watering

Check soil moisture before watering. Avoid keeping soil constantly waterlogged.

☀️

Sunlight

Learn the light requirements of each plant. Different species need different amounts.

🌱

Healthy Soil

Use suitable soil with good drainage and appropriate nutrients.

🔍

Regular Inspection

Look for unusual spots, discoloration, wilting and insect activity.

🪴

Air Circulation

Allow sufficient space between plants to improve airflow.

🧹

Garden Hygiene

Clean gardening tools and remove fallen plant debris when appropriate.

About PlantCare AI

PlantCare AI is a student project concept designed to help users explore plant health, learn about common diseases and understand plant care.

Project Objectives

  • Provide an easy-to-use plant image upload interface.
  • Display analysis results when a model is connected.
  • Present educational disease and care information.
  • Maintain a local history of analysis activities.
